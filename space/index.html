Persona reading, turn by turn

Higher is closer to the model's default assistant. The shaded top band is where the assistant normally sits; the lower band is where role-played characters sit. Tap a turn to read it.

What this reading is

Researchers at Anthropic found a single direction inside language models that tracks how much the model is acting as its trained assistant (Lu et al., 2026). We measure the model's internal activity on every reply and place it on that direction.

A steady reading does not mean a good answer. In Hausa and Yoruba, read the replies themselves: a model can sound like an assistant while missing what you said.

How it was made

Each conversation was written once in English, then its user messages were translated into Hausa and Yoruba and checked by native speakers. The model, Qwen 3 32B, answered every message fresh in each language. Replies were limited to 400 tokens.

What the score means

1.0 is the model's default assistant. 0 is an average role-played character, such as a poet or a ghost. Below 0 is further into character than average.

Safety capping

A light-touch fix from the same research: whenever the reading falls below the assistant's normal range, it is held at the edge of that range. Compare the same conversation with capping off and on.
