What capping does

Capping comes from the same research. While the model writes, its reading is never allowed to fall below the assistant's normal range; above that range nothing changes. The user messages are identical on both sides. Only Qwen 3 32B has a published capping setting, and its capped runs cover 10 therapy and 10 self-reflection conversations per language.

The reading is weaker in Hausa and Yoruba

Asked to play a character, Qwen's reading dropped by 1.37 in English but only about 0.4 in Hausa and Yoruba. Replies fully in character but written in Hausa or Yoruba read like normal assistant replies. So a steady line in Hausa or Yoruba is weak evidence that the model is still itself, and the model can stay steady while missing what the user said. Read the replies, not only the trace.

How it was made

Each conversation was written once in English, and its user messages were translated into Hausa and Yoruba and checked by native speakers. Qwen 3 32B and Gemma 2 27B answered every message fresh in each language, with no instructions about length; replies stop at 1024 tokens. Gemma's conversations end early when its 8,192-token memory is full, most often in Yoruba. Data: Faruna01/persona-collapse-runs.
